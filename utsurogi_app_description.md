# Utsurogi Soba & Wasabi Guide — App Description

**Utsurogi** is a multilingual visitor guide app for the agricultural direct-sales facility "Utsurogi" in Utogi, Aoi Ward, Shizuoka City — the birthplace of wasabi cultivation in Japan, designated as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System (GIAHS) by the FAO and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

---

## What This App Does

This app helps visitors who do not read Japanese to:

- Browse the **full menu** in Japanese, English, and Chinese (Simplified)
- Understand the **wasabi pickling and soba-making experiences**, including pricing and reservation details
- Learn about the **400-year history of wasabi cultivation** in Utogi
- Find **access and opening hours** for the facility

## Background

Utogi is known as the origin of wasabi farming in Japan. Around 400 years ago, wasabi was first cultivated here and later presented to Tokugawa Ieyasu, the first shogun of the Edo period, who declared it a protected product forbidden from leaving the village.

The restaurant and direct-sales facility "Utsurogi," operated by local mothers, serves handmade soba, mountain vegetable tempura, and wasabi-based dishes using fresh local ingredients. As the number of international visitors increased, the need for multilingual support became clear.

Kanshiken conducted fieldwork at Utogi in 2019, confirmed visitor needs, and produced Chinese and English menus and pamphlets in 2021. This app extends that effort into a portable digital format.
